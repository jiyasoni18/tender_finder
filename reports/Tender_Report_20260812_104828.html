5. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS & COMPLIANCE

Compliance with stringent specifications and quality assurance is critical:

  • General Standards: Indian Railway Signal Engineering Manual (IRSEM).
  • Material Specifications: Adherence to various RDSO, IRS, IS, and TEC specifications (e.g., IRS-S-40/84, RDSO/SPN/189/2004, RDSO/SPN/177/2012, IS 694/2010, TEC GR/OFC-02/03, etc.). Specific versions and amendments must be followed.
  • Approved Vendors: Procurement of many items (e.g., terminals, HASSDAC components, cables, fuses) must be from RDSO approved sources. OEM supporting certificates required for certain cables (e.g., CAT-6).
  • Brand Preference/Equivalents: Specific brands mentioned for certain items (e.g., Wago/Phoenix/Elmex for terminals, Fluke/DI-LOG for multimeter, Motwani/Philips for insulation tester, G.G.Tronics for HASSDAC, D-Link/Amp/Systimax for cables, Asian/Burger/Nerolac for paints). Equivalent or better quality is generally accepted if specified.
  • Inspection Protocols:
    • Consignee Inspection: For most supply items, PVC conduits, warning boards, earth electrodes, bricks, cable laying, painting.
    • RDSO Inspection: For HASSDAC coil assemblies, axle detectors, non-deteriorating fuses, railway signaling cables.
    • RITES Inspection: Specifically for Double Walled Corrugated (DWC) HDPE pipe and its accessories.
  • Execution Guidelines: All installation and execution work must be carried out as per Railway Engineer's instructions, relevant drawings (e.g., NR/S&T/CON/1.1/97-A), and standard practices.
  • Quality Assurance: Materials for painting must be ISI marked (IS:15489:2004). Meggering of cables before and after laying, with submission of meggering sheets by the contractor.
